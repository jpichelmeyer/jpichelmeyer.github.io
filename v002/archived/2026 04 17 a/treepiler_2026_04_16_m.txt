Theory notes

	AST: The set of all abstract syntax trees (ASTs)
		Not partially ordered by default (just a set, no inherent structure)
		Becomes a partially ordered set when equipped with a partial order relation 
		<= (leq), which is:
			Reflexive		T1 subtree of T1 for all T1 in AST
			Antisymmetry	IF[ (T1 subtree of T2) AND (T2 subtree of T1) ] THEN (T1=T2)
			Transitivity    T1 subtree of T2 and T2 a subtree of T3 implies T1 subtree of T3
