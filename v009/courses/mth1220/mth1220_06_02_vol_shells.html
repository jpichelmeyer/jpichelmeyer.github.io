Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 The region under $y=x^2$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  2. 2 The region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  3. 3 The region under $y=\dfrac{1}{x}$ on $[1,3]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  4. 4 The region under $y=\sin(x^2)$ on $[0,\sqrt{\pi}]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume. (Disks would require solving $y=\sin(x^2)$ for $x$ — not elementary!)
  5. 5 The region under $y=x^3$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  6. 6 The region under $y=\cos(x)$ on $[0,\pi/2]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  7. 7 The region between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  8. 8 The region between $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  9. 9 The region between $y=4-x^2$ and $y=0$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  10. 10 The region between $y=2x$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  11. 11 The region under $y=x^2$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the line $x=3$. Use shells to find the volume.
  12. 12 The region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$ is revolved about the line $x=-1$. Use shells to find the volume.
  13. 13 The region bounded by $x=y^2$ and $x=4$ (for $y\ge 0$) is revolved about the $x$-axis. Use shells (in terms of $y$) to find the volume.
  14. 14 The region bounded by $y=x^3$, $x=0$, and $y=8$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Use shells (in terms of $y$) to find the volume.
  15. 15 Explain briefly why the shell method (rather than disks/washers) is the natural choice for revolving the region under $y=\sin(x^2)$ on $[0,\sqrt{\pi}]$ about the $y$-axis.
  16. 16 The region under $y=e^{x}$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  17. 17 The region under $y=\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  18. 18 Set up, but do not evaluate, the shell-method integral for the region between $y=x^3$ and $y=x$ (for $x\ge 0$) revolved about the $y$-axis.
  19. 19 The region under $y=x^2$ on $[0,3]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find the volume.
  20. 20 A vase is modeled by revolving the region under $y=x^{1/3}$ on $[0,1]$ about the $y$-axis. Use shells to find its volume.
