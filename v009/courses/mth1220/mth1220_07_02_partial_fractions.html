Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{2x+1}{(x-1)(x+2)}\,dx$.
  2. 2 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x^2-1}\,dx$.
  3. 3 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{3x-1}{x^2-x-2}\,dx$.
  4. 4 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{5}{x(x+5)}\,dx$.
  5. 5 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{3}{(x-4)(x-2)(x+1)}\,dx$.
  6. 6 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x+1}{x^2(x-1)}\,dx$.
  7. 7 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{(x-1)^2(x+1)}\,dx$.
  8. 8 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x^2+1}{x(x-1)^2}\,dx$.
  9. 9 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{2x^2-x+4}{x^3+4x}\,dx$.
  10. 10 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x^3+1}{x^2-1}\,dx$. (Divide first — the degree of the numerator isn't less than the denominator's.)
  11. 11 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_2^3 \frac{2x+1}{(x-1)(x+2)}\,dx$.
  12. 12 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_2^3 \frac{1}{x^2-1}\,dx$.
  13. 13 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^2 \frac{5}{x(x+5)}\,dx$.
  14. 14 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_2^3 \frac{x+1}{x^2(x-1)}\,dx$.
  15. 15 Write the correct partial fraction decomposition \emph{form} (with unknown constants $A,B,C$ — do not solve for them) for $\dfrac{x^2+3}{(x-1)(x+2)(x-5)}$.
  16. 16 Write the correct partial fraction decomposition form for $\dfrac{x+4}{(x-3)^2(x+1)}$.
  17. 17 Write the correct partial fraction decomposition form for $\dfrac{3x^2-x+1}{x(x^2+4)}$.
  18. 18 Before applying partial fractions to $\dfrac{x^2+1}{x^2-4}$, what algebraic step is required, and why?
  19. 19 A chemical reaction's rate leads to $\dfrac{dx}{dt}=k(a-x)(b-x)$ with $a\ne b$. After separating variables, the left side becomes $\displaystyle\int \frac{dx}{(a-x)(b-x)}$. Decompose this integrand into partial fractions (treat $a,b$ as constants).
  20. 20 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x}{x^2-5x+6}\,dx$.

Answers

  1. 1.$\log{\left(x^{2} + x - 2 \right)} + C$
  2. 2.$\frac{\log{\left(x - 1 \right)}}{2} - \frac{\log{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{2} + C$
  3. 3.$\frac{5 \log{\left(x - 2 \right)}}{3} + \frac{4 \log{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{3} + C$
  4. 4.$\log{\left(x \right)} - \log{\left(x + 5 \right)} + C$
  5. 5.$\frac{3 \log{\left(x - 4 \right)}}{10} - \frac{\log{\left(x - 2 \right)}}{2} + \frac{\log{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{5} + C$
  6. 6.$- 2 \log{\left(x \right)} + 2 \log{\left(x - 1 \right)} + \frac{1}{x} + C$
  7. 7.$- \frac{\log{\left(x - 1 \right)}}{4} + \frac{\log{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{4} - \frac{1}{2 x - 2} + C$
  8. 8.$\log{\left(x \right)} - \frac{2}{x - 1} + C$
  9. 9.$\log{\left(x \right)} + \frac{\log{\left(x^{2} + 4 \right)}}{2} - \frac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\frac{x}{2} \right)}}{2} + C$
  10. 10.$\frac{x^{2}}{2} + \log{\left(x - 1 \right)} + C$
  11. 11.$\log{\left(\frac{5}{2} \right)} \approx 0.9163$
  12. 12.$- \frac{\log{\left(2 \right)}}{2} + \frac{\log{\left(3 \right)}}{2} \approx 0.2027$
  13. 13.$\log{\left(\frac{12}{7} \right)} \approx 0.5390$
  14. 14.$- \frac{1}{6} + \log{\left(\frac{16}{9} \right)} \approx 0.4087$
  15. 15.$\dfrac{A}{x-1}+\dfrac{B}{x+2}+\dfrac{C}{x-5}$
  16. 16.$\dfrac{A}{x-3}+\dfrac{B}{(x-3)^2}+\dfrac{C}{x+1}$
  17. 17.$\dfrac{A}{x}+\dfrac{Bx+C}{x^2+4}$
  18. 18.Polynomial long division first, since the numerator's degree ($2$) is not less than the denominator's ($2$) — partial fractions only decomposes a \emph{proper} rational function.
  19. 19.$\dfrac{1}{(a-x)(b-x)} = \dfrac{1}{b-a}\left(\dfrac{1}{a-x}-\dfrac{1}{b-x}\right)$
  20. 20.$3 \log{\left(x - 3 \right)} - 2 \log{\left(x - 2 \right)} + C$
