Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 The region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  2. 2 The region under $y=x^2$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  3. 3 The region under $y=\dfrac{1}{x}$ on $[1,3]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  4. 4 The region under $y=e^{x}$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  5. 5 The region under $y=\cos(x)$ on $[0,\pi/2]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  6. 6 The region under $y=\sqrt{4-x^2}$ on $[-2,2]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume, and check your answer against the formula for the volume of a sphere.
  7. 7 The region between $y=x$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  8. 8 The region between $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  9. 9 The region between $y=4$ and $y=x^2$ (its own bounds) is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  10. 10 The region between $y=2x$ and $y=x^2$ (its own bounds, for $x\ge 0$) is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  11. 11 The region between $y=x^2$ and $y=4$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Find the volume.
  12. 12 The region bounded by $y=x^3$, the $y$-axis, and $y=8$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Find the volume.
  13. 13 The region bounded by $y=\sqrt{x}$, $y=0$, and $x=4$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Find the volume.
  14. 14 The region between $x=y$ and $x=y^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis. Find the volume.
  15. 15 The base of a solid is the region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$. Cross-sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis are squares. Find the volume.
  16. 16 The base of a solid is the region under $y=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,4]$. Cross-sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis are equilateral triangles. Find the volume.
  17. 17 The base of a solid is the disk $x^2+y^2\le 1$. Cross-sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis are squares. Find the volume.
  18. 18 Set up, but do not evaluate, the integral for the volume when the region bounded by $y=x^2$ and $y=x+2$ is revolved about the $x$-axis.
  19. 19 The region between $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis. Find the volume.
  20. 20 The base of a solid is the triangle bounded by $x+y=2$ and the coordinate axes. Cross-sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis are semicircles with diameter stretching across the base. Find the volume.
