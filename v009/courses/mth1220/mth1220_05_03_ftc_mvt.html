Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{2} (x^3-2x+1)\,dx$.
  2. 2 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin(x)\,dx$.
  3. 3 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi/4} \sec^2(x)\,dx$.
  4. 4 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{e} \frac{1}{x}\,dx$.
  5. 5 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\ln 2} e^{x}\,dx$.
  6. 6 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{4} \sqrt{x}\,dx$.
  7. 7 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} (x^4-3x^2)\,dx$.
  8. 8 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{1}^{4} \frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx$.
  9. 9 Find $g'(x)$ if $\displaystyle g(x)=\int_2^x \sqrt{1+t^3}\,dt$.
  10. 10 Find $g'(x)$ if $\displaystyle g(x)=\int_0^{x^2} \sin(t)\,dt$.
  11. 11 Find $g'(x)$ if $\displaystyle g(x)=\int_1^{x^3} \ln(t)\,dt$.
  12. 12 Find $g'(x)$ if $\displaystyle g(x)=\int_x^{x^2} \cos(t)\,dt$.
  13. 13 Find $g'(x)$ if $\displaystyle g(x)=\int_x^{5} t^2\,dt$.
  14. 14 State the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, Part 1, in your own words (what does it say about the derivative of an accumulation function?).
  15. 15 State the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, Part 2 (the Evaluation Theorem), in your own words.
  16. 16 Find the average value of $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,3]$, then find a value $c$ in $[0,3]$ satisfying the Mean Value Theorem for Integrals.
  17. 17 Find the average value of $f(x)=x^2+1$ on $[0,2]$, then find $c$ in $[0,2]$ with $f(c)$ equal to that average.
  18. 18 Find the average value of $f(x)=\sin(x)$ on $[0,\pi]$, then set up (but do not simplify further than an inverse trig expression) the equation for $c$.
  19. 19 Find the average value of $f(x)=x^3$ on $[0,2]$, then find the real value $c$ in $[0,2]$ with $f(c)$ equal to that average.
  20. 20 A particle has velocity $v(t)=t^2-4t+5$ (m/s). Use the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus to find its displacement over $[0,3]$.

  11. 11.$g'(x)=3x^2\ln(x^3)=9x^2\ln x$ (chain rule)
  12. 12.$g'(x)=2x\cos(x^2)-\cos(x)$ (Leibniz rule — differentiate each limit separately)
  13. 13.$g'(x)=-x^2$ (flip the limits to make $x$ the upper bound, picking up a minus sign)
  14. 14.If $g(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ with $f$ continuous, then $g$ is differentiable and $g'(x)=f(x)$ — differentiation undoes integration.
  15. 15.If $F$ is any antiderivative of a continuous $f$ on $[a,b]$, then $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b)-F(a)$ — a definite integral can be evaluated from an antiderivative, with no limit process needed.

  17. 17.Average value $=\dfrac{7}{3}$; solving $x^2+1=\dfrac{7}{3}$ on $[0,2]$ gives $c=\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
  18. 18.Average value $=\dfrac{2}{\pi}\approx 0.6366$; $c=\arcsin\!\left(\dfrac{2}{\pi}\right)\approx 0.6901$.
