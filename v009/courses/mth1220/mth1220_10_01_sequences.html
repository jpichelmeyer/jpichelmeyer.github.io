Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Find a formula for $a_n$ given the first terms $\dfrac12,\ \dfrac23,\ \dfrac34,\ \dfrac45,\ \dots$
  2. 2 Find a formula for $a_n$ given the first terms $1,\ -\dfrac12,\ \dfrac14,\ -\dfrac18,\ \dots$
  3. 3 Find a formula for $a_n$ given the first terms $2,\ 5,\ 10,\ 17,\ 26,\ \dots$
  4. 4 Find a formula for $a_n$ given the first terms $1,\ \dfrac14,\ \dfrac19,\ \dfrac1{16},\ \dots$
  5. 5 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{3n^2+1}{n^2-2}$.
  6. 6 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{2n+3}{n^3+1}$.
  7. 7 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac12\right)^n$.
  8. 8 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n$.
  9. 9 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n}{\sqrt{n^2+1}}$.
  10. 10 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\cos(n)}{n}$. (Squeeze Theorem.)
  11. 11 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n^2}{e^n}$.
  12. 12 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n!}{n^n}$.
  13. 13 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{n}$, and justify with the Squeeze Theorem.
  14. 14 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\ln(n)}{n}$.
  15. 15 Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{3n+(-1)^n}{n+2}$.
  16. 16 Is the sequence $a_n=\dfrac{n}{n+1}$ increasing, decreasing, or neither? Is it bounded? What can you conclude about convergence?
  17. 17 Is the sequence $a_n=(-1)^n$ bounded? Is it monotonic? Does it converge?
  18. 18 Given $-\dfrac{1}{n^2}\le a_n\le \dfrac{1}{n^2}$ for all $n$, find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n$.
  19. 19 State the Monotone Convergence Theorem for sequences.
  20. 20 Explain the difference between a sequence and an infinite series, and give a simple example of each.

  5. 5.$3$
  6. 6.$0$

  9. 9.$1$

  13. 13.$0$, since $-\dfrac1n\le\dfrac{(-1)^n}{n}\le\dfrac1n$ and both bounds $\to 0$.
  14. 14.$0$
  15. 15.$3$
  16. 16.Increasing, since $a_{n+1}-a_n=\dfrac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}>0$; bounded above by $1$. By the Monotone Convergence Theorem, it converges (to $1$).
  17. 17.Bounded (between $-1$ and $1$), but not monotonic — it oscillates, so the Monotone Convergence Theorem doesn't apply, and in fact the sequence diverges (it never settles on one value).
  18. 18.$0$, by the Squeeze Theorem.
  19. 19.If a sequence $\{a_n\}$ is monotonic (always increasing or always decreasing) and bounded, then it converges.
  20. 20.A sequence is an ordered list of numbers, e.g. $a_n=\tfrac1n$ gives $1,\tfrac12,\tfrac13,\dots$; a series is the sum of the terms of a sequence, e.g. $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\tfrac1n$, and its convergence depends on whether the sequence of \emph{partial sums} converges.
