Answers

  1. 1.$\operatorname{asin}{\left(\frac{x}{3} \right)} + C$
  2. 2.$\frac{x \sqrt{4 - x^{2}}}{2} + 2 \operatorname{asin}{\left(\frac{x}{2} \right)} + C$
  3. 3.$- \frac{x \sqrt{1 - x^{2}}}{2} + \frac{\operatorname{asin}{\left(x \right)}}{2} + C$
  4. 4.$-\dfrac{\sqrt{9-x^2}}{9x}+C$
  5. 5.$\frac{\operatorname{atan}{\left(\frac{x}{3} \right)}}{3} + C$
  6. 6.$\ln\!\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+4}\right)+C$
  7. 7.$\dfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2+9}-\dfrac{9}{2}\ln\!\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+9}\right)+C$
  8. 8.$\sqrt{x^2+1}-\ln\!\left(\dfrac{1+\sqrt{x^2+1}}{x}\right)+C$
  9. 9.$\frac{x}{4 \sqrt{x^{2} + 4}} + C$
  10. 10.$\ln\!\left(x+\sqrt{x^2-9}\right)+C$
  11. 11.$\sqrt{x^2-4}-2\arccos\!\left(\dfrac{2}{x}\right)+C$
  12. 12.$\dfrac{\sqrt{x^2-1}}{x}+C$
  13. 13.$\dfrac{x^2}{3}\sqrt{x^2+4}-\dfrac{8}{3}\sqrt{x^2+4}+C$
  14. 14.$\pi \approx 3.142$
  15. 15.$\frac{\pi}{6} \approx 0.5236$
  16. 16.$\frac{\pi}{12} \approx 0.2618$
  17. 17.$- \frac{9 \log{\left(1 + \sqrt{2} \right)}}{2} + \frac{9 \sqrt{2}}{2} \approx 2.398$
  18. 18.$x=a\sin\theta$; the radical becomes $\sqrt{a^2-a^2\sin^2\theta}=a\cos\theta$.
  19. 19.$x=a\tan\theta$; the radical becomes $\sqrt{a^2+a^2\tan^2\theta}=a\sec\theta$.
  20. 20.With $x=4\tan\theta$, $dx=4\sec^2\theta\,d\theta$, $\sqrt{x^2+16}=4\sec\theta$: $\displaystyle\int \frac{4\sec^2\theta\,d\theta}{16\tan^2\theta\cdot 4\sec\theta}=\frac{1}{16}\int \frac{\sec\theta}{\tan^2\theta}\,d\theta$
