Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Suppose $\int_1^5 f(x)\,dx = 12$ and $\int_1^5 g(x)\,dx = 6$. Evaluate $\int_1^5 \big(2f(x)-3g(x)\big)\,dx$.
  2. 2 Suppose $\int_0^4 f(x)\,dx = 9$ and $\int_2^4 f(x)\,dx = 5$. Evaluate $\int_0^2 f(x)\,dx$.
  3. 3 Suppose $\int_{-1}^{3} f(x)\,dx = 7$. Evaluate $\int_{3}^{-1} f(x)\,dx$.
  4. 4 Suppose $\int_2^6 f(x)\,dx = 10$ and $\int_2^6 g(x)\,dx = -3$. Evaluate $\int_2^6 \big(5g(x)-f(x)\big)\,dx$.
  5. 5 Evaluate $\int_5^5 \big(x^3-7x+2\big)\,dx$ using a property of the definite integral, without antidifferentiating.
  6. 6 Suppose $\int_1^3 f(x)\,dx = 4$, $\int_3^8 f(x)\,dx = -2$, and $\int_1^8 g(x)\,dx = 6$. Evaluate $\int_1^8\big(3f(x)+g(x)\big)\,dx$.
  7. 7 If $2\le f(x)\le 5$ for all $x$ in $[1,4]$, use a comparison property of integrals to give the tightest lower and upper bounds you can on $\int_1^4 f(x)\,dx$.
  8. 8 If $-1\le f(x)\le 3$ for all $x$ in $[0,6]$, give the tightest lower and upper bounds on $\int_0^6 f(x)\,dx$ that the Comparison Property guarantees.
  9. 9 Explain, using a property of definite integrals, why $\int_0^{2\pi}\sin(x)\,dx = 0$ without computing an antiderivative.
  10. 10 Sketch $f(x)=3-x$ on $[0,5]$ and use the areas of the resulting triangle above the axis and the triangle below the axis to evaluate $\int_0^5 (3-x)\,dx$.
  11. 11 Evaluate $\int_{-2}^{2}\sqrt{4-x^2}\,dx$ by recognizing the region as a semicircle.
  12. 12 Evaluate $\int_{-3}^{3} x^3\,dx$ using symmetry, without antidifferentiating.
  13. 13 Evaluate $\int_{0}^{4} |x-2|\,dx$ by splitting the region into two triangles.
  14. 14 A function $f$ is measured at $x=0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8$ with values $f(x) = 2,3,5,4,6,7,5,4,3$ respectively. Use the Midpoint Rule with $n=4$ (pairing consecutive readings) to estimate $\int_0^8 f(x)\,dx$.
  15. 15 Water flows into a tank at rates (in L/min) of $4, 6, 9, 7, 5$ measured at $t=0,2,4,6,8$ minutes. Use the Midpoint Rule with the two middle readings ($n=2$, width $4$) to estimate the total liters that entered in $8$ minutes.
  16. 16 Estimate $\int_1^9 g(x)\,dx$ using the Midpoint Rule with $n=4$, given $g(2)=1.5,\ g(4)=2.5,\ g(6)=3,\ g(8)=2$, each representing the midpoint of a width-$2$ subinterval.
  17. 17 Express $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^n \left(3+\frac{5i}{n}\right)^4\cdot\frac{5}{n}$ as a definite integral. (Do not evaluate.)
  18. 18 Express $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^n \tan\!\left(\frac{\pi i}{4n}\right)\cdot\frac{\pi}{4n}$ as a definite integral. (Do not evaluate.)
  19. 19 State, in words, what the sign of $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ tells you when $f$ dips below the $x$-axis somewhere on $[a,b]$.
  20. 20 If $\int_0^{10} f(x)\,dx = 40$ represents total distance-related net displacement and $f(x)>0$ for all $x$, what can you conclude about the object's motion on $[0,10]$?

Answers

  1. 1.$2(12)-3(6)=6$
  2. 2.$9-5=4$
  3. 3.$-7$
  4. 4.$5(-3)-10=-25$
  5. 5.$0$
  6. 6.$3(4+(-2))+6 = 12$
  7. 7.$6 \le \int_1^4 f(x)\,dx \le 15$
  8. 8.$-6 \le \int_0^6 f(x)\,dx \le 18$
  9. 9.$\sin$ is odd about $x=\pi$ on $[0,2\pi]$ (equal positive and negative area), so the signed areas cancel exactly.
  10. 10.$\frac{5}{2}$
  11. 11.$2 \pi \approx 6.283$
  12. 12.$0$ ($x^3$ is odd, and the interval is symmetric about $0$)
  13. 13.$4$
  14. 14.$M_4 = 1\cdot\big(f(1)+f(3)+f(5)+f(7)\big) = 3+4+7+4 = 18$
  15. 15.$M_2 = 4\big(f(2)+f(6)\big) = 4(6+7) = 52 \text{ L}$
  16. 16.$M_4 = 2(1.5+2.5+3+2) = 18$
  17. 17.$\displaystyle\int_3^8 x^4\,dx$
  18. 18.$\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4} \tan x\,dx$
  19. 19.The integral gives the \emph{net} signed area: regions below the axis subtract from regions above it, rather than being ignored.
  20. 20.The object moves in one direction the entire time (velocity never negative), so net displacement equals total distance traveled, namely $40$.
