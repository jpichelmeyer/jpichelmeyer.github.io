Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int x^5\,dx$.
  2. 2 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int (3x^2-4x+7)\,dx$.
  3. 3 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \sqrt{x}\,dx$.
  4. 4 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x^3}\,dx$.
  5. 5 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int e^{x}\,dx$.
  6. 6 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x}\,dx$.
  7. 7 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \left(4x^3-6\sqrt{x}+\frac{2}{x}\right)dx$.
  8. 8 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \frac{x^2+1}{x}\,dx$.
  9. 9 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \sin(x)\,dx$.
  10. 10 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \cos(x)\,dx$.
  11. 11 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \sec^2(x)\,dx$.
  12. 12 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \sec(x)\tan(x)\,dx$.
  13. 13 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx$.
  14. 14 Find the general antiderivative: $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx$.
  15. 15 Verify by differentiation that $F(x)=x\ln(x)-x$ is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\ln(x)$.
  16. 16 Verify by differentiation that $F(x)=\tfrac{1}{3}\sin^3(x)$ is an antiderivative of $f(x)=\sin^2(x)\cos(x)$.
  17. 17 An object moves with velocity $v(t)=3t^2-2t$ (m/s) and $s(0)=5$ m. Find the position function $s(t)$.
  18. 18 An object has acceleration $a(t)=6t$ (m/s$^2$) and $v(0)=-1$ m/s. Find the velocity function $v(t)$.
  19. 19 A company's marginal cost is $C'(x)=3x^2-12x+15$ dollars per unit, and fixed costs are $C(0)=200$. Find the total cost function $C(x)$.
  20. 20 Solve the initial value problem $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2x-3$, $y(1)=4$.

Answers

  1. 1.$\frac{x^{6}}{6} + C$
  2. 2.$x^{3} - 2 x^{2} + 7 x + C$
  3. 3.$\frac{2 x^{\frac{3}{2}}}{3} + C$
  4. 4.$- \frac{1}{2 x^{2}} + C$
  5. 5.$e^{x} + C$
  6. 6.$\log{\left(x \right)} + C$
  7. 7.$- 4 x^{\frac{3}{2}} + x^{4} + 2 \log{\left(x \right)} + C$
  8. 8.$\dfrac{x^2}{2}+\ln|x|+C$
  9. 9.$- \cos{\left(x \right)} + C$
  10. 10.$\sin{\left(x \right)} + C$
  11. 11.$\tan(x)+C$
  12. 12.$\sec(x)+C$
  13. 13.$\arctan(x)+C$
  14. 14.$\arcsin(x)+C$
  15. 15.$F'(x)=\ln(x)+x\cdot\frac{1}{x}-1=\ln(x)+1-1=\ln(x)=f(x)$. \checkmark
  16. 16.$F'(x)=\sin^2(x)\cos(x)=f(x)$ by the chain rule. \checkmark
  17. 17.$s(t)=t^3-t^2+5$
  18. 18.$v(t)=3t^2-1$
  19. 19.$C(x)=x^3-6x^2+15x+200$
  20. 20.$y(x)=x^2-3x+6$
