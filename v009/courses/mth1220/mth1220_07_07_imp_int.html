Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^2}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  2. 2 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  3. 3 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty} e^{-x}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  4. 4 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty} \frac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  5. 5 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^{3/2}}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  6. 6 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{\infty} x\,e^{-x^2}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  7. 7 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  8. 8 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{1}{x}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  9. 9 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{1}{x^2}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  10. 10 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{3} \frac{1}{(3-x)^{1/3}}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  11. 11 Determine whether $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \ln(x)\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.
  12. 12 Explain why $\displaystyle\int_0^{2} \frac{1}{(x-1)^2}\,dx$ cannot be evaluated by naively finding an antiderivative and plugging in the bounds. What is the actual value of the integral?
  13. 13 Use the Comparison Test to determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^3+1}\,dx$ converges.
  14. 14 Use the Comparison Test to determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1+\sin(x)}{x^2}\,dx$ converges.
  15. 15 Use the Comparison Test to determine whether $\displaystyle\int_2^{\infty} \frac{1}{\ln(x)}\,dx$ converges.
  16. 16 Use the Comparison Test to determine whether $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx$ converges.
  17. 17 For what values of $p$ does $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^p}\,dx$ converge?
  18. 18 For what values of $p$ does $\displaystyle\int_0^{1} \frac{1}{x^p}\,dx$ converge?
  19. 19 For a probability density function to be valid, $\int_0^\infty f(x)\,dx$ must equal $1$. Verify this for the exponential density $f(x)=3e^{-3x}$.
  20. 20 For $p>1$, derive a general formula for $\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^p}\,dx$ in terms of $p$ (and confirm it diverges for $p\le 1$).

Answers

  1. 1.$1$
  2. 2.Diverges
  3. 3.$1$
  4. 4.$\frac{\pi}{2} \approx 1.571$
  5. 5.$2$
  6. 6.$\frac{1}{2}$
  7. 7.$2$
  8. 8.Diverges
  9. 9.Diverges
  10. 10.$\frac{3 \cdot 3^{\frac{2}{3}}}{2} \approx 3.120$
  11. 11.$-1$
  12. 12.The integrand blows up at $x=1$, an \emph{interior} point of $[0,2]$ — the integral must be split into $\int_0^1+\int_1^2$, and each piece diverges to $+\infty$ on its own (the integrand is always positive, so a finite negative \"answer\" from blind FTC is a red flag). The integral diverges.
  13. 13.Converges — $0<\dfrac{1}{x^3+1}<\dfrac{1}{x^3}$ for $x\ge 1$, and $\int_1^\infty x^{-3}\,dx$ converges ($p=3>1$).
  14. 14.Converges — $0\le\dfrac{1+\sin x}{x^2}\le\dfrac{2}{x^2}$ for all $x$, and $\int_1^\infty 2x^{-2}\,dx$ converges.
  15. 15.Diverges — $\ln x < x$ for $x>1$, so $\dfrac{1}{\ln x}>\dfrac{1}{x}$, and $\int_2^\infty \dfrac{1}{x}\,dx$ diverges.
  16. 16.Converges — $e^{-x^2}\le e^{-x}$ for $x\ge 1$, and $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx$ converges.
  17. 17.$p>1$
  18. 18.$p<1$
  19. 19.$1$
  20. 20.$\displaystyle\int_1^{\infty} x^{-p}\,dx = \frac{1}{p-1}$ for $p>1$; for $p\le 1$ the limit defining the improper integral is infinite, so it diverges.
