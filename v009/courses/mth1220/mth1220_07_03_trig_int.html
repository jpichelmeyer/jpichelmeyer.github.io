Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin^3(x)\,dx$.
  2. 2 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cos^5(x)\,dx$.
  3. 3 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin^2(x)\cos^2(x)\,dx$.
  4. 4 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin^4(x)\,dx$.
  5. 5 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin^2(x)\,dx$.
  6. 6 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cos^2(x)\,dx$.
  7. 7 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \tan^3(x)\sec(x)\,dx$.
  8. 8 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \tan^2(x)\sec^4(x)\,dx$.
  9. 9 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \tan^3(x)\,dx$.
  10. 10 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sec^3(x)\,dx$.
  11. 11 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sec^4(x)\,dx$.
  12. 12 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin(3x)\cos(5x)\,dx$.
  13. 13 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin(2x)\sin(4x)\,dx$.
  14. 14 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cos(x)\cos(3x)\,dx$.
  15. 15 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cot^3(x)\csc^2(x)\,dx$.
  16. 16 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}\sin^3(x)\,dx$.
  17. 17 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^2(x)\cos^2(x)\,dx$.
  18. 18 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4}\tan^3(x)\sec(x)\,dx$.
  19. 19 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^3(x)\cos(x)\,dx$.
  20. 20 Summarize the strategy for evaluating $\displaystyle\int \sin^m(x)\cos^n(x)\,dx$: what do you do when at least one of $m,n$ is odd, versus when both are even?

Answers

  1. 1.$\frac{\cos^{3}{\left(x \right)}}{3} - \cos{\left(x \right)} + C$
  2. 2.$\frac{\sin^{5}{\left(x \right)}}{5} - \frac{2 \sin^{3}{\left(x \right)}}{3} + \sin{\left(x \right)} + C$
  3. 3.$\dfrac{x}{8}-\dfrac{\sin(4x)}{32}+C$
  4. 4.$\dfrac{3x}{8}-\dfrac{\sin(2x)}{4}+\dfrac{\sin(4x)}{32}+C$
  5. 5.$\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{\sin(2x)}{4}+C$
  6. 6.$\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{\sin(2x)}{4}+C$
  7. 7.$\dfrac{\sec^3(x)}{3}-\sec(x)+C$
  8. 8.$- \frac{2 \sin{\left(x \right)}}{15 \cos{\left(x \right)}} - \frac{\sin{\left(x \right)}}{15 \cos^{3}{\left(x \right)}} + \frac{\sin{\left(x \right)}}{5 \cos^{5}{\left(x \right)}} + C$
  9. 9.$\dfrac{\tan^2(x)}{2}+\ln|\cos(x)|+C$
  10. 10.$\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(\sec(x)\tan(x)+\ln|\sec(x)+\tan(x)|\Big)+C$
  11. 11.$\tan(x)+\dfrac{\tan^3(x)}{3}+C$
  12. 12.$\frac{5 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} \sin{\left(5 x \right)}}{16} + \frac{3 \cos{\left(3 x \right)} \cos{\left(5 x \right)}}{16} + C$
  13. 13.$- \frac{\sin{\left(2 x \right)} \cos{\left(4 x \right)}}{3} + \frac{\sin{\left(4 x \right)} \cos{\left(2 x \right)}}{6} + C$
  14. 14.$- \frac{\sin{\left(x \right)} \cos{\left(3 x \right)}}{8} + \frac{3 \sin{\left(3 x \right)} \cos{\left(x \right)}}{8} + C$
  15. 15.$- \frac{1 - 2 \sin^{2}{\left(x \right)}}{4 \sin^{4}{\left(x \right)}} + C$
  16. 16.$\frac{4}{3}$
  17. 17.$\frac{\pi}{16} \approx 0.1964$
  18. 18.$\frac{2}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \approx 0.1953$
  19. 19.$\frac{1}{4}$
  20. 20.If $m$ or $n$ is odd, peel off one factor of that function, convert the remaining even power using $\sin^2+\cos^2=1$, and substitute $u=$ the other function. If both are even, repeatedly apply the half-angle identities $\sin^2x=\tfrac{1-\cos2x}{2}$, $\cos^2x=\tfrac{1+\cos2x}{2}$ to reduce the powers.
