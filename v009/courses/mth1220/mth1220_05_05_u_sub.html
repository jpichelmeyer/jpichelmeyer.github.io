Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int 2x(x^2+1)^4\,dx$.
  2. 2 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x^2\sqrt{x^3+1}\,dx$.
  3. 3 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin(x)\cos^3(x)\,dx$.
  4. 4 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cos(x)\,e^{\sin(x)}\,dx$.
  5. 5 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{x}{x^2+1}\,dx$.
  6. 6 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sec^2(2x)\,dx$.
  7. 7 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \cos(3x)\,dx$.
  8. 8 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int (2x+1)\,e^{x^2+x}\,dx$.
  9. 9 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x\ln(x)}\,dx$.
  10. 10 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int x\,e^{-x^2}\,dx$.
  11. 11 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^4 \sqrt{2x+1}\,dx$.
  12. 12 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x\sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx$.
  13. 13 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} \sin(x)\,e^{\cos(x)}\,dx$.
  14. 14 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{2} \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{x^4+9}}\,dx$.
  15. 15 What substitution $u=\ ?$ would you use to evaluate $\displaystyle\int \tan(x)\sec^2(x)\,dx$, and why?
  16. 16 What substitution $u=\ ?$ would you use to evaluate $\displaystyle\int \frac{\ln(x)}{x}\,dx$, and why?
  17. 17 Use symmetry to evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{2} x^3\cos(x)\,dx$ without antidifferentiating.
  18. 18 Use symmetry to evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}\big(x^4+3x^2\big)\,dx$ by doubling the integral over $[0,1]$ (the integrand is even).
  19. 19 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sin^3(x)\cos(x)\,dx$.
  20. 20 Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^{e^2} \frac{1}{x\sqrt{\ln x}}\,dx$.

Answers

  1. 1.$\dfrac{(x^2+1)^5}{5}+C$ (let $u=x^2+1$)
  2. 2.$\dfrac{2}{9}(x^3+1)^{3/2}+C$ (let $u=x^3+1$)
  3. 3.$-\dfrac{\cos^4(x)}{4}+C$ (let $u=\cos x$)
  4. 4.$e^{\sin(x)}+C$ (let $u=\sin x$)
  5. 5.$\dfrac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+1)+C$ (let $u=x^2+1$)
  6. 6.$\dfrac{1}{2}\tan(2x)+C$ (let $u=2x$)
  7. 7.$\dfrac{1}{3}\sin(3x)+C$ (let $u=3x$)
  8. 8.$e^{x^2+x}+C$ (let $u=x^2+x$)
  9. 9.$\ln|\ln(x)|+C$ (let $u=\ln x$)
  10. 10.$-\dfrac{1}{2}e^{-x^2}+C$ (let $u=-x^2$)
  11. 11.$\frac{26}{3}$
  12. 12.$\frac{1}{3}$
  13. 13.$-1 + e \approx 1.718$
  14. 14.$1$
  15. 15.$u=\tan(x)$, since $du=\sec^2(x)\,dx$ is already present — the integral becomes $\int u\,du = \tfrac{1}{2}\tan^2(x)+C$.
  16. 16.$u=\ln(x)$, since $du=\dfrac{1}{x}dx$ is already present — the integral becomes $\int u\,du = \tfrac{1}{2}\ln^2(x)+C$.
  17. 17.$0$ — the integrand is odd ($(-x)^3\cos(-x) = -x^3\cos x$) and the interval is symmetric about $0$.
  18. 18.$\frac{12}{5}$
  19. 19.$\dfrac{\sin^4(x)}{4}+C$ (let $u=\sin x$)
  20. 20.$2 \sqrt{2} \approx 2.828$
