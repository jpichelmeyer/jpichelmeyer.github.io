Practice Examples

Work out your answers to the following questions on paper, then check your answers below.

  1. 1 Determine whether $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} 3\left(\frac12\right)^n$ converges, and find its sum if so.
  2. 2 Determine whether $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(-\frac13\right)^n$ converges, and find its sum if so.
  3. 3 Determine whether $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2^n}{3^{n+1}}$ converges, and find its sum if so.
  4. 4 Determine whether $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{5}{4^n}$ converges, and find its sum if so.
  5. 5 Determine whether $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac32\right)^n$ converges.
  6. 6 Express the repeating decimal $0.\overline{7}$ as a geometric series and find its exact fractional value.
  7. 7 Find the sum of the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+1)}$.
  8. 8 Find the sum of the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{(n+1)(n+2)}$.
  9. 9 Find the sum of the telescoping series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n(n+2)}$. (Hint: $\dfrac{1}{n(n+2)}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1n-\dfrac1{n+2}\right)$.)
  10. 10 Show that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \ln\!\left(\frac{n}{n+1}\right)$ diverges, even though it telescopes.
  11. 11 Use the $n$th-Term Test to show $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n}{n+1}$ diverges.
  12. 12 Use the $n$th-Term Test to show $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{2n^2+1}{n^2+3}$ diverges.
  13. 13 Use the $n$th-Term Test to show $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \cos\!\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)$ diverges.
  14. 14 Explain why the $n$th-Term Test cannot be used to conclude that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac1n$ converges, even though $\dfrac1n\to 0$.
  15. 15 Does $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^3}$ converge or diverge? State the general $p$-series rule you used.
  16. 16 Does $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}$ converge or diverge?
  17. 17 Does $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n}$ (the harmonic series) converge or diverge?
  18. 18 A ball is dropped from $10$ m and rebounds to $60\%$ of its previous height on each bounce. Set up a geometric series for the total vertical distance traveled, and find its sum.
  19. 19 State the necessary condition every convergent series $\sum a_n$ must satisfy (the contrapositive of this is the $n$th-Term Test).
  20. 20 Explain, using partial sums, what it means for an infinite series $\sum a_n$ to \emph{converge}.

Answers

  1. 1.Converges (ratio $\tfrac12$); sum $=6$.
  2. 2.Converges (ratio $-\tfrac13$); sum $=\dfrac34$.
  3. 3.Converges; sum $=\dfrac23$.
  4. 4.Converges; sum $=\dfrac{20}{3}$.
  5. 5.Diverges — ratio $\tfrac32>1$.
  6. 6.$\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{7}{10^n}=\dfrac{7}{9}$
  7. 7.$1$
  8. 8.$\dfrac12$
  9. 9.$\dfrac34$
  10. 10.The partial sum is $S_N=\ln\!\left(\dfrac{1}{N+1}\right)\to -\infty$ — telescoping doesn't guarantee convergence.
  11. 11.$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n}{n+1}=1\ne 0$, so the series diverges.
  12. 12.$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{2n^2+1}{n^2+3}=2\ne 0$, so the series diverges.
  13. 13.$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\cos\!\left(\frac1n\right)=\cos(0)=1\ne 0$, so the series diverges.
  14. 14.The $n$th-Term Test only detects divergence when the terms don't go to $0$; when they do, the test is inconclusive. The harmonic series $\sum \tfrac1n$ is the classic example of terms $\to 0$ with a series that still diverges.
  15. 15.Converges — a $p$-series converges exactly when $p>1$, and here $p=3>1$.
  16. 16.Diverges — this is a $p$-series with $p=\tfrac12\le 1$.
  17. 17.Diverges — $p$-series with $p=1$.
  18. 18.Total distance $=10+2\big(10(0.6)+10(0.6)^2+\cdots\big)=10+2\cdot\dfrac{6}{1-0.6}=10+30=40$ m.
  19. 19.If $\sum a_n$ converges, then $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n=0$.
  20. 20.The sequence of partial sums $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n$ must itself converge to some finite limit $S$ as $N\to\infty$; that limit $S$ is defined to be the sum of the series.
