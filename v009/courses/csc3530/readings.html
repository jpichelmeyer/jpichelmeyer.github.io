Each lesson comes with one or two short readings, usually three to six pages. They are chosen to support or push against the lesson. Sometimes the author agrees with the knowledge-based approach, and sometimes the reading argues the opposite. Read them before the class where the topic is introduced, and come ready to say how the reading connects to the lesson. The weekly quiz draws on them.

The Three Books

  • Hofstadter, D. & Sander, E. (2013). Surfaces and Essences: Analogy as the Fuel and Fire of Thinking. Basic Books.
  • McShane, M., Nirenburg, S., & English, J. (2024). Agents in the Long Game of AI: Computational Cognitive Modeling for Trustworthy, Hybrid AI. MIT Press. Open access (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).
  • Bennett, M. S. (2023). A Brief History of Intelligence: Evolution, AI, and the Five Breakthroughs That Made Our Brains. Mariner Books.
  • Hofstadter & Sander argue that analogy-making and categorization are the same process and that it sits at the core of all thought. Their book is the cognitive-science backbone for Module 2, and their letter-string "Copycat" domain is a close cousin of ARC.
  • McShane, Nirenburg & English describe a working knowledge-based cognitive architecture (OntoAgent) and its language-endowed agents. It is the closest thing to "this course, built for real." The whole book is open access from MIT Press if you want to read beyond the excerpts.
  • Bennett tells the story of intelligence through brain evolution, comparing each stage with an AI technique. He is our reality check: what does biology say about the mechanisms we build?

How to Read for This Course

  1. Find the claim. Every excerpt makes one main claim about minds or machines. Write it in one sentence.
  2. Find the evidence. An experiment? An anecdote? A working system? A thought experiment?
  3. Connect it to the lesson. Does the reading support the technique we are building, explain why people do something similar, or warn about where it breaks?
  4. Connect it to your agent. One sentence: what would you change in your ARC agent if the author is right?
