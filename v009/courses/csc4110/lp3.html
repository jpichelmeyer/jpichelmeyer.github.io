Lab Practical 3 is the Find, Identify, Mark mission run by your team on the class hub, with faults injected while it runs. It is graded twice: as a team (did the mission work, how accurately, and how did it recover) and individually (does your robot meet its part of the contract, and can you explain its behaviour under a fault). The mission, the coordinator and the contract are those of the mission integration lesson; nothing new is introduced here.

Before the Day

  • Test plan, submitted 48 hours before: one row per requirement and per fault from the mission lesson's fault list, with your team's own recovery limits filled in, the method, and the number of trials you ran at home. A plan with failed rows, explained, is a good plan.
  • Frozen code on every robot, the version you tested; the commit or file hashes listed in the plan.
  • Measured poses for every robot, entered in each stat/role and in the fuser's layout.
The Stage 3 robots a team brings: two Scouts, a Watcher, a Pointer, a Signaler and a Sentinel (or a Stabilizer in a team of six).

How It Runs

The order of the practical. The class Pi runs the world service and the mission coordinator; the logger records everything. The phase log and the database are the evidence for every timing in the rubric.

The Runs

  1. Set-up (10 minutes). Robots placed at their measured poses, powered from cold. The examiner checks world/roster: every role present and online. The phase must reach SEARCH without anyone touching a laptop.
  2. Run 1, clean. The examiner places the tube and sets the beacon colour out of your sight. The team runs the mission to DONE. Recorded: time to DONE, the reported position against the true one (measured by ruler), the reported colour.
  3. Runs 2 and 3, with faults. A new position and colour each run. During each run the examiner injects two faults from the list below, at moments of their choosing, and notes the time of each. Recorded: whether the mission still reaches DONE, the recovery time for each fault (from the phase log, as in Lab 12), and whether any wrong mark occurred.
The fault list (the same as the mission lesson's). Your test plan states your recovery limits; you are graded against your own limits, so state limits you can meet and have tested.

The Orals

Each team member, for two minutes: show your robot's part of the contract on the broker (its topics, fields and rates), explain what your robot did during one of the injected faults (from the log, not from memory), and answer one question from the Unit 3 paper-practice sets.

Rubric

The LP3 rubric. T rows are shared by the team; I rows are individual. A wrong mark (the Pointer marking a position the operator did not approve, or the wrong target) forfeits the T4 points for that run.

Checkpoint

Before the practical, make sure your team can:

  1. Bring the whole team from cold to SEARCH with nobody touching a laptop.
  2. Run the mission to DONE three times in a row on your own broker, with stand-ins where needed.
  3. Inject every fault on the list and measure its recovery from the phase log.
  4. Explain, for each robot, what it does when the network, a teammate or the hub disappears.
