A system you have only seen working when everything goes right is a system you do not yet understand. This lab breaks things on purpose. You will add the week’s resilience features to your Pico, and then your instructor will inflict four failures on it while you watch what it does.

What Your Pico Must Do

  1. Connect with a last will: offline (retained) on lab/<id>/stat/online, and publish online after connecting.
  2. Restore its output state from a file in flash at boot, and apply it before connecting.
  3. Publish its output state retained, at QoS 1, on lab/<id>/stat/fan.
  4. Obey lab/<id>/cmd/fan with idempotent commands (on / off).
  5. Recover from any failure with backoff and jitter, re-subscribing and re-announcing each time.
  6. Publish telemetry with a sequence number, and keep a bounded backlog while away.
The Lab 6 failure drills. Each one breaks something on purpose; your Pico passes only if it behaves as the right-hand columns describe.

Part 2. The Drills

Watch the Pico’s serial output and, on the Pi, mosquitto_sub -v -t 'lab/+/stat/#' together. Your instructor will run each drill; record what you see.

One drill over time. The broker goes down at 30 s and returns at 90 s. A resilient Pico retries with growing gaps, recovers soon after the broker is back, and sends what it kept while away.

Solutions

Give each exercise an honest try before opening its solution.

Warm Up

Exercise 1: Predict the Drill

The dashboard should show offline about 45 seconds after the WiFi dropped (1.5 × 30), when the broker publishes the will. When WiFi returns, the Pico reconnects, publishes online, and the dashboard flips back.

Exercise 2: Why Not Retry Fast?

It wastes power and keeps the radio busy, and it hammers the broker the moment it restarts, from every student at once. It can also starve the rest of the program of time. Backoff with jitter avoids all three.

Intermediate

Exercise 3: Measure Recovery Time
main.py MicroPython
lost_at = None # in the except OSError branch of the main loop: lost_at = ticks_ms() connect_broker() client.publish(TELE, payload) if lost_at is not None: took = ticks_diff(ticks_ms(), lost_at) client.publish(RECOVERY, str(took).encode()) lost_at = None

This measures from detection to recovery on the Pico’s own clock. It does not include the time before the failure was noticed, which is why the keep-alive also matters.
